T–03 · The story

They made it. Now they have to stop it.

Atomic Fall is a high-octane sci-fi thriller about four brilliant engineers forced to fight the ultimate power they created.

After a devastating betrayal unleashes tyranny and triggers a catastrophic countdown, the team must race through a fractured, cyberpunk city to undo the weapon before it consumes all of reality.

A relentless story of technological horror, flawed heroes, and the ultimate cost of saving the world.
