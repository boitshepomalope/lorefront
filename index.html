FAQ

Questions

What is A+ content?

The image section Amazon shows under "From the Publisher" on a book's page, below the description. It's where you can show readers the whole series at a glance.

Does Amazon charge for it?

No. A+ content is free for KDP authors. You only pay for the design.

Who puts it on my book pages?

You add it in your KDP account in a few minutes, using the images and the guide I send. Amazon reviews A+ content before it goes live, which can take up to seven business days.

Can one design go on every book in the series?

Yes. Amazon lets you apply the same A+ content to several books at once, ebook and paperback editions included.

What if I don't like the concept?

Then you've lost nothing. The concept is free and you only pay once you approve.
